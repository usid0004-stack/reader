Want a Jarvis-like voice? Only the basic voices are installed. The free "Enhanced" and "Premium" voices sound far more natural. Daniel or Oliver (Enhanced, British) is the closest thing to Jarvis.
  1. Open System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content
  2. Next to System Voice, open the menu and choose Manage Voices…
  3. Under English (UK), tick Daniel (Enhanced), Oliver (Enhanced) or Jamie (Premium) and wait for the download
  4. Reload this page. They will appear at the top of the voice list marked HQ.
